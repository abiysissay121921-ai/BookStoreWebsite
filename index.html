About Arganon Book Store

Spreading Knowledge Across Generations

Located near the historic 5 Kilo area in Addis Ababa, Arganon Book Store is dedicated to providing high-quality spiritual, historical, and educational literature to enthusiastic readers and scholars.

Our Mission

To make spiritual and educational literature readily accessible, fostering deeper faith, knowledge, and an enduring reading culture.

Our Vision

To be the premier bookstore destination that empowers minds and preserves cultural and spiritual heritage through trusted books.
